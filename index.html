About Sweethome S&D

A practical link between supply and the shelf.

Many independent food businesses buy in small quantities from several sources, and many suppliers want steady commercial buyers. We exist to make that movement of food more dependable, by coordinating orders and delivery in one place.

We work business to business. We are not a shop, a restaurant or a consumer delivery service.

Reliability

We plan around agreed schedules, and we tell people early when something changes.

Clear communication

Plain answers on prices, timing and responsibilities, before work starts.

Fair relationships

We aim for supplier and buyer relationships that both sides want to keep.

Honest about scope

We say we can do something only when we can. Growth follows capacity.

Food supply and distribution

Order coordination

Scheduled and last-mile delivery
