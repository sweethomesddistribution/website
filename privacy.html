Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Sweethome S&D Distribution Inc. ("Sweethome S&D", "we", "us") handles personal information that you send us through this website. We keep it short, because we collect very little.

Who we are

Sweethome S&D Distribution Inc. is a federal corporation (Federal Corporation No. 1837371-0) with its registered office at 3390 South Service Road, Burlington, Ontario, L7N 3J5, Canada. You can reach us at admin@sweethomesddistribution.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email:

  • your name and company
  • your email address, and your phone number if you give it
  • whether you are a supplier, a business customer or something else
  • the message you write

We do not ask for payment details, and you should not send them to us through the form.

How we use it

We use this information to read and reply to your message, and to discuss working together. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it for us

To run this website we rely on a few service providers, which may handle your information as part of their service:

  • A form relay service receives what you type into the contact form and forwards it to our email address.
  • Google Workspace hosts our business email, where the message arrives.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit.

These providers may be located outside Canada, so your information may be processed in other countries.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics tools. The fonts are served from this website itself.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to keep a sensible business record of our dealings. We then delete them or keep only what we are required or entitled to keep.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing admin@sweethomesddistribution.com. We handle personal information in line with Canadian privacy law.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date above.
